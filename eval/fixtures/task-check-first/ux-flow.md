# UX Flow — Preview Service

<!-- sentinel: ux-phase-1-must-survive -->

## Phase 1 — Resize the panel

1. The person drags the panel edge.
2. Thumbnails move to their new positions and settle.
3. Nothing in the panel asks for input while thumbnails settle.

## Renaming

- To rename a thumbnail: open its context menu, choose Rename, type, press Enter.
- Escape cancels and keeps the old name.
