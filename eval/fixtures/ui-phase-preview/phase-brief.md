# Phase Brief — Signal Desk — Phase 2

## Phase Goal

Analysts can open the Insight Inbox and act on a launch signal without learning a new interaction pattern.

## Design Principle

Reuse the established visual language; this phase needs no new component variants.

## Phase Scope

- Compose an **Insight Inbox** from the existing `InsightCard` and `PrimaryButton` components.
- Show the real signal title **Monday launch signal** and its source, Northstar Weekly.
- Keep the primary action visually consistent with Phase 1.

## Exit Criteria

1. Insight Inbox
   - Open the inbox: the Monday launch signal is visible in the established card treatment.
   - Act on the signal: the existing primary action remains clear.
